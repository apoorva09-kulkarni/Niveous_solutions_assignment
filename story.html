An AI analyst prepares the file. A credit officer decides.

How a mid-sized Indian bank can use a lakehouse and AI agents to review personal loans faster and more consistently, with a human always in charge of the decision.

The situation today

Priya applies for a ₹5 lakh personal loan. To review her file, an officer opens documents in one place, customer records in another, and credit bureau and identity checks in a third. They re-type details, chase missing papers and compare everything by hand. Files stall, and two officers can reach different answers on the same file.

The lakehouse: one complete view per application

Every fact about Priya sits together, with its source. The officer, the agent and the auditor all read from the same record.

What the bank gains: no copy-pasting between systems, a clear trail of which data each decision used, and one foundation that agents can read from.

Part 2: How the agent uses that information

One Loan Assessment Agent works through the file in four steps. An agent is software that reads information, uses tools such as a policy lookup or a calculator, and produces a result. We use one well-defined agent rather than many shallow ones.

  1. 1
    Check the documentsUses: uploaded documents and the required-documents listProduces: complete or missing list
  2. 2
    Look up the policyUses: the bank’s lending policyProduces: the rules that apply to this loan
  3. 3
    Check credit and affordabilityUses: credit report, income, existing EMIsProduces: score, new EMI, share of income going to EMIs
  4. 4
    Flag risks and recommendUses: results of steps 1 to 3Produces: risk flags and a recommendation, each with its reason
↓

Hand-off to a human

The credit officer reads the reasons and the data behind them, then approves, rejects or asks for more information. The agent cannot approve a loan. Sanction and payout stay locked until the officer approves.

Built around RBI’s digital lending rules

We checked these in the RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025. RBI sets the guardrails; the bank sets its own decision rules.

Rule (paragraph)Where it shows up in the demo
Record age, occupation and income before lending (para 7)The affordability step
Give the borrower a Key Fact Statement and signed documents (para 8)The sanction step
Pay out only to the borrower’s own account (para 9)The disbursement step
Cooling-off period of at least one day (para 10)After sanction

What is real and what is simulated

Working in the demo: the policy rules, the EMI and affordability calculations, the risk flags, the officer approval step.

Simulated: the agent’s reasoning (scripted logic, not a live AI model), and the credit bureau and identity checks (made-up responses).

Not built: a live BigQuery connection. It is the target platform and is shown on the architecture page.
