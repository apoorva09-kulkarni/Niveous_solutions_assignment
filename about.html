About this project

A point-of-view demo for a Business Analyst (Data and AI Presales) assignment: how AI agents and a lakehouse could help a mid-sized Indian bank process personal loans, with a credit officer making every final decision.

What is here

The demo

An officer dashboard with 12 synthetic applicants, the agent’s steps, risk flags, reasons and the sanction-to-disbursement flow.

Approach

The business story: one place for the data, and an agent that prepares the file for the officer.

Architecture

The layers from data sources to screens, marking what works, what is simulated and what is only a target.

How it was made

The author researched the process using public sources, mainly RBI’s Digital Lending Directions, 2025, and designed the loan journey, the agent steps and the policy assumptions. Claude, an AI assistant, helped draft research, generate the synthetic data and write the code. The author tested the demo and reviewed the results. The research note and AI usage log are in the repository.

Limits

All data is synthetic, the agent is scripted logic, outside services are simulated, and policy values are assumptions. See the FAQ for details.
